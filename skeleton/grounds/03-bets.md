# Bets

`B-NNN` — which requirement rests on which hypotheses. The join lives here
and only here: a requirement never says what it stands on, so the register
says what stands on it, and the other direction is computed.

`all_of` is a chain — refute any one and the ground is gone. `any_of` is a
set of alternatives, and the strongest carries the requirement. Two
independent sets of alternatives are two bets naming the same requirement,
and the checker asks whether that was deliberate.
