# Forms

`S-NNN` — what the parts this system is made of are made of inside.

Each record names the one part it is inside, the obligations of that part it answers for, and — where they are real — the symbols it is, the operations anything of it answers to, the state it owns alone, and the forms it is composed of.

A form is designed from the obligations its part carries, never read off the code: that is the order this framework runs in, and it is why a form can be written, closed against those obligations and read by everybody before a line of its code exists.
Write a form where there is a boundary somebody could cross without noticing, not where there is a class.

The format is `srs-dd/arch.md`, under *What a part is made of inside*; the map is generated into `90-map.md`.
Nothing obliges a part to hold a form: the grain is adopted a part at a time, and a part holding none reads as it did before anybody wrote one.
